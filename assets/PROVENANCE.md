## Graphics

- Terrain textures: generated procedurally at runtime (`crates/rr-app/src/tex.rs`), GPL-3.0-or-later as code.
- Sprites: drawn in code (`crates/rr-app/src/sprites.rs`): settlers, animals, flags, fields, wares; roads textured procedurally (`tex.rs`).
- `assets/art/trees/*.png`: rendered by our own SDF ray marcher (`tools/art/trees.py`, built on `tools/art/prerender.py`; procedural shapes and materials, no external input). CC-BY-SA-4.0.
- Concept art from an AI image tool: `docs/images/`, see `docs/ART.md`.

## Maps

- `data/maps/europe_mask.txt`: land/sea/lake raster of Europe made with `tools/europe_mask.py` from Natural Earth 1:50m land and lakes (public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/, via https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector). Mountain ranges, climate and start regions in `crates/rr-mapgen/src/europe.rs` are our own, from real-world geography. Nothing is taken from the original game's Europe map.
- `data/maps/{mediterranean,northamerica,fareast,north}_mask.txt`: made the same way from Natural Earth (public domain) with `tools/europe_mask.py W H LON0 LON1 LAT0 LAT1`; ranges, climates and starts in `crates/rr-mapgen/src/world.rs` are our own.
- `assets/art/menu_village.jpg`: a screenshot of this game (the Europe mission at minute 30, made headless with `tools/uitest.sh` on 2026-10-25), the menu's backdrop since art director round 4; same licence as the game's own art.
- `assets/art/menu_bg.jpg`: painted on 2026-10-23 by an AI image tool from our own text prompt (no reference images, the game not named). Released under CC-BY-SA-4.0 with the open art pack. The prompt, in full:
  > a wide painted illustration (16:9) of a peaceful medieval European river valley seen from a hill: a small settlement of timber and stone houses with red roofs, a stone watchtower with a blue banner, winding dirt roads with little flags, tiny workers carrying goods, a woodcutter near a forest, wheat fields, mountains with snow in the far distance, warm late-afternoon light, rich painterly 1990s fantasy box-art style, no text, no logos, no letters.
- `assets/art/buildings/{woodcutter,headquarters}.png`: painted on 2026-10-23 by an AI image tool from our own text prompts (no reference images, the original game not named), keyed and scaled with `tools/art/post.py`. CC-BY-SA-4.0.
- The other Roman summer pictures `assets/art/buildings/<building>.png` (`"source": "prompt"` in the manifest): painted on 2026-10-26 by an AI image tool from the same kind of original prompt (no reference images, the original game not named), keyed and scaled with `tools/art/post.py`. CC-BY-SA-4.0. The generated originals stay on the machine that made them (pictures are not in git). The prompt shape, with one description per building:
  > a single small building for a strategy game, isolated: *the building*. Camera: three-quarter frontal view from about 30 degrees above, the front door facing the viewer slightly to the left. Style: soft pre-rendered 1990s 3D look, smooth shading, no black outlines, no pixel art, light from the upper left, warm natural colors, realistic proportions. The building stands directly on the background: no ground plate, no grass patch, no shadow on the ground, no other objects, no text. Background: one solid flat pure green color #00FF00 everywhere around the building.
- The Roman campaign's holy places `assets/art/holy/<place>.png` (`"source": "prompt"`): the same, with "a single small sacred place for a strategy game set in ancient Rome" in place of "a single small building", painted on 2026-10-26. Not used by the game yet (docs/CAMPAIGN-ROME.md). CC-BY-SA-4.0.
- The ships `assets/art/ships/<ship_heading[_loaded]>.png` (`"source": "prompt"`), a Roman merchant ship (corbita) in six headings, empty and loaded: painted on 2026-10-28/29 by an AI image tool from our own text prompt (the original game not named). The east-facing one came from the prompt alone; for the other eleven that picture, our own, was attached as the only reference ("exactly the same ship, only its heading changes"), so all twelve show one ship. Cut at the waterline and scaled with `tools/art/post_ship.py`. CC-BY-SA-4.0. The prompt shape:
  > a single Roman merchant ship (a corbita) for a strategy game, isolated: a broad, round-bellied wooden hull of warm brown planks with a dark tarred band along the waterline; a tall sternpost curving up and forward, ending in a carved swan's head; a small wooden cabin on the stern deck; two large steering oars hanging at the stern, one on each side; a main mast amidships with a horizontal yard and a large plain off-white linen square sail, slightly bellied by the wind, with thin vertical brail lines; a small foremast leaning forward over the bow with a small square foresail (artemon); *an empty hold, or amphorae kept low*. Camera: three-quarter view from about 30 degrees above, *the heading*. Style: soft pre-rendered 1990s 3D look, smooth shading, no black outlines, no pixel art, light from the upper left, warm natural colors, realistic proportions. No water, no waves, no shadow, no other objects, no text, no flags, no emblems on the sail. Background: fully transparent.
- `assets/icon/roads-and-realms-*.png` (the launcher's and the window's icon): painted on 2026-10-26 by an AI image tool from our own prompt (a sandstone castle on a hill, no reference images), cut to a rounded square with a shadow and scaled with ImageMagick. CC-BY-SA-4.0.
