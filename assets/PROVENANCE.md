## Graphics

- Terrain textures: generated procedurally at runtime (`crates/rr-app/src/tex.rs`), GPL-3.0-or-later as code.
- Sprites: drawn in code (`crates/rr-app/src/sprites.rs`): settlers, animals, flags, fields, wares; roads textured procedurally (`tex.rs`).
- `assets/art/trees/*.png`: rendered by our own SDF ray marcher (`tools/art/trees.py`, built on `tools/art/prerender.py`; procedural shapes and materials, no external input). CC-BY-SA-4.0.
- agy (Gemini) concept art: `docs/images/`, see `docs/ART.md`.

## Maps

- `data/maps/europe_mask.txt`: land/sea/lake raster of Europe made with `tools/europe_mask.py` from Natural Earth 1:50m land and lakes (public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/, via https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector). Mountain ranges, climate and start regions in `crates/rr-mapgen/src/europe.rs` are our own, from real-world geography. Nothing is taken from the original game's Europe map.
- `data/maps/{mediterranean,northamerica,fareast,north}_mask.txt`: made the same way from Natural Earth (public domain) with `tools/europe_mask.py W H LON0 LON1 LAT0 LAT1`; ranges, climates and starts in `crates/rr-mapgen/src/world.rs` are our own.
- `assets/art/menu_village.jpg`: a screenshot of this game (the Europe mission at minute 30, made headless with `tools/uitest.sh` on 2026-10-25), the menu's backdrop since art director round 4; same licence as the game's own art.
- `assets/art/menu_bg.jpg`: generated with agy (Google Gemini image generation) on 2026-10-23 from an original text prompt (no reference images, game not named); prompt in `art-gen/menu/PROMPT.txt`. Released under CC-BY-SA-4.0 with the open art pack.
- `assets/art/buildings/{woodcutter,headquarters}.png`: generated with agy (Gemini image model, 2026-10-23) from original text prompts (`art-gen/bld/list.txt`, `gen.sh`; no reference images, the original game not named), keyed and scaled with `tools/art/post.py`. CC-BY-SA-4.0.
- The other Roman summer pictures `assets/art/buildings/<building>.png` (`"source": "codex"` in the manifest): generated on 2026-10-26 with OpenAI's image model through the Codex CLI's image tool, from the same original prompts (`art-gen/bld/gen_codex.sh`, `run_codex.sh`; no reference images, the original game not named), keyed and scaled with `tools/art/post.py`. CC-BY-SA-4.0. The generated originals stay in `art-gen/bld/` on the machine that made them (pictures are not in git).
- `assets/icon/roads-and-realms-*.png` (the launcher's and the window's icon): painted on 2026-10-26 by OpenAI's image model through the Codex CLI from an original prompt (a sandstone castle on a hill, no reference images), cut to a rounded square with a shadow and scaled with ImageMagick. CC-BY-SA-4.0.
- All other `assets/art/buildings/*.png` (`"source": "prerender"` in the manifest: the other peoples and the winter pictures): rendered by our own SDF ray marcher `tools/art/prerender.py` (procedural shapes and materials, no external input). CC-BY-SA-4.0.
