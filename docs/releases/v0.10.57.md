## A living map, and a Roman ship

**The map lives.** Details that are only drawn, never part of the map, the save or the game (the same map always looks the same):

- gusts rolling through trees, reeds, tufts and grain, carrying leaves, sand or snow;
- on the water: foam rolling onto the beaches, sparkles, jumping fish, ducks and lily pads on still water, mist over rivers and ponds, white water where a river falls;
- on the land: worn earth before the doors, a darker forest floor, flowers in drifts, mushrooms beside trees, rocks in outcrops, footprints in snow and sand, the trampled ground where soldiers fought;
- life: birds startled from the trees, flocks with their shadows, gulls at harbours, butterflies, fireflies over swamps, embers over lava;
- work you can see: wood chips, stone dust, sparks, and the firelight of a working smithy, bakery or brewery.

Options → **Calm map** keeps what lies still and stops everything that moves, for players whom motion bothers.

**Ships.** A Roman merchant ship, a corbita, painted in its own picture for each of the six headings, empty and loaded with amphorae. Your colour is a band across its sail and a pennant at the masthead; a wake trails behind it.

**Fixes**

- No crash when a carrier's place on its road lies past the road's end, or when a castle has been lost.
- Saves are checked harder when they load.
- At most seven players (more are clamped, with a message); small maps are safe.
- The map editor's raise and lower brush works on tiny maps.
- A lost castle no longer counts as one of its realm's warehouses (wares could be sent to it).
- Loading a save onto a smaller map no longer crashes the game.

**Compatibility:** saves of 0.9.56 load and play on. One rule of play changed (a lost castle no longer counts as a warehouse), so replays and LAN games don't mix with 0.9.56: everyone in a LAN game needs 0.10.57.

**Downloads:** Linux (x86_64) and Windows (x86_64) below. On Windows, the game starts with OpenGL and falls back to DirectX 12 by itself.
