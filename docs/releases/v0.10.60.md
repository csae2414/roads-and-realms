## Holding an island won across the sea

**Sea attacks from the posts that can send the most.** A few small posts of yours on the enemy's island, with no soldier to spare, made every attack there one on foot, and your big posts by a harbour were never asked. Now an attack goes by sea when the posts by a harbour can send more soldiers than those on foot, and from the harbour whose posts can send the most.

**Soldiers for your posts on an island without a warehouse.** Posts you won on another island got no soldiers, because no warehouse of yours stood there. Now an idle ship brings them from the military buildings by one of your harbours, sets them ashore at the nearest harbour spot, and they move in. Whether the strongest or the weakest go first follows your military settings.

**A harbour on land you conquered.** An expedition can land on a harbour spot of your own land on another coast, not only on free land. Clicking such a spot says so when no builder can reach it. A tree on the spot no longer keeps an expedition away: the expedition fells it.

**Soldiers don't die when you pull down a building far from a warehouse.** They move into a post of yours nearby that still has room. Only when none is within 50 steps do they wander off as before.

**Compatibility:** saves of 0.10.59 load and play on. The rules of play changed, so replays and LAN games don't mix with 0.10.59: everyone in a LAN game needs 0.10.60.

**Downloads:** Linux (x86_64) and Windows (x86_64) below. On Windows, the game starts with OpenGL and falls back to DirectX 12 by itself.
