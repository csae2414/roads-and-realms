## Islands: war and trade across the sea

**Attacks across the sea.** A rival who holds a whole island of their own, with no border to yours, can now be attacked. A sea attack may target any enemy building you have seen once, even if it is out of sight now. Your soldiers still land on a harbour spot no more than 15 nodes from the target. Attacks over land still need the target in sight. If an attack isn't possible, the building's window says why: no harbour spot near enough, no harbour of yours on that sea, no free ship, or no soldiers to spare in your posts by the harbour.

**A geologist or scout for another island.** If you send one to a flag that none can walk to, a ship brings one from a harbour across the sea. Send him again once he has landed.

**Warehouse settings across the sea.** "Take in" fetches a ware or settler by ship from a harbour across the sea when nothing on its own island has it. A harbour set to "move out" ships its stock to a harbour across the sea when no warehouse on its own island takes it.

The computer players use the new sea attacks too, so keep your coastal posts manned.

**Compatibility:** saves of 0.10.57 load and play on. The rules of play changed, so replays and LAN games don't mix with 0.10.57: everyone in a LAN game needs 0.10.58.

**Downloads:** Linux (x86_64) and Windows (x86_64) below. On Windows, the game starts with OpenGL and falls back to DirectX 12 by itself.
