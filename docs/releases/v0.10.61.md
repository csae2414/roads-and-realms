## Harbours on islands you won

**A harbour where you have no warehouse.** Place a harbour on a harbour spot of your own land on another island, one you won across the sea: your nearest harbour on that sea gathers a builder, boards and stones, and a ship brings them over. No expedition needed, and only the harbour's own materials. The building site's window says what is coming. Harbour spots on your land without a warehouse are marked with an anchor.

**Expeditions.** An expedition whose harbour has no builder sends for one from your other warehouses, instead of waiting for ever. A harbour spot clicked with no expedition ready offers to prepare one for that spot; it sails there once loaded. You can cancel an expedition while it is still in its harbour: its boards, stones and builder go back into the stock. The harbour window says when your warehouses hold too few stones or boards for it.

**Soldiers by sea.** A message says when soldiers set sail for one of your posts. An enemy building out of sight can be attacked by sea under the same rule as one in sight.

**Compatibility:** saves of 0.10.60 load and play on. The rules of play changed, so replays and LAN games don't mix with 0.10.60: everyone in a LAN game needs 0.10.61.

**Downloads:** Linux (x86_64) and Windows (x86_64) below. On Windows, the game starts with OpenGL and falls back to DirectX 12 by itself.
