## Defenders who keep fighting

**A defender who wins out in the open stays in the fight.** When your building is attacked, soldiers from your posts nearby rush out to meet the attackers on their way. Until now, one who won his fight walked straight home, and the building often fell before he was back. Now he takes on the next attacker of the same building close by first, and goes home only when none is left. An attacker he comes for stops and waits for him.

A building still falls once an attacker stands at its flag and nobody is left inside, even while its own soldiers are still outside. How many soldiers rush out is set in the military settings.

You still choose how many soldiers go on an attack, from one to all available, and whether the strongest or the weakest go.

**Fixed:** an attacker waiting for a defender who never came (his building burned meanwhile) now walks on instead of standing there.

**Compatibility:** saves of 0.10.58 load and play on. The rules of play changed, so replays and LAN games don't mix with 0.10.58: everyone in a LAN game needs 0.10.59.

**Downloads:** Linux (x86_64) and Windows (x86_64) below. On Windows, the game starts with OpenGL and falls back to DirectX 12 by itself.
